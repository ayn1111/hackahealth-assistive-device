# One Ring, Many Tools: Modular Assistive Holder (HackaHealth Madrid 2026)

A modular, 3D-printable device that lets a person hold and use everyday tools with one thumb. Designed and built in three days at HackaHealth España 2026 (Madrid edition, 20–22 November 2026), a hackathon where teams co-design solutions with a person with a disability.

Team: Challenge 1

## The challenge

Our challenger wanted to shave on his own, comfortably and safely, without depending on anyone else. The device had to be worn on his left thumb, the part of his hands with the most mobility.

Key requirements:

- Worn on the left hand
- Water resistant
- Quick and easy to put on and take off, without needing force from the other hand
- Firm grip on the tool, even under pressure
- Portable

## The solution

The device has three parts:

1. **A semi-open, flexible thumb ring**, which slips on and off easily without help.
2. **A tool module** that holds the utensil. The razor module closes around the handle and increases the contact surface, giving a firm grip while staying flexible.
3. **A magnetic connector with rotation and locking** (twist-lock) that joins the ring and the module, so tools can be swapped quickly.

The device met the original goal: our challenger could shave on his own. Because the tool module can be swapped, it also let him brush his teeth, eat with a spoon and write.

## How we got there

- **Friday:** first ideas, including two rings with a detachable joint, different ways to hold the thumb and the razor, and a magnet to secure the connection.
- **Saturday:** prototypes and tests of several connections: a sliding dovetail rail, two press-fit joints and a rotating joint with a lock.
- **Sunday:** final design, combining the thumb ring, the tool module and the magnetic twist-lock connector.

Two problems shaped the final design:

- **Ring size.** Measurements of the thumb were not exact, so we printed several rings with millimetre differences. Our challenger tried each one and chose the most comfortable and secure.
- **Grip on the razor.** We enlarged the contact surface with the handle and closed the module around it, and designed a separate module for razors with wider handles.

## Next steps

- Adapt the razor module to different razor sizes
- Make the spoon attachment more stable, as its own separate module
- Make the device even easier to put on
